libredwg's DWG decoder (llvmfuzz ASan target): a heap-buffer-overflow read of a single byte occurs while the object decoder processes an LWPOLYLINE entity's fields, specifically inside the helper that peeks the next small field-format tag without advancing the cursor. Root cause: a low-level helper shared by every entity's field-by-field decoding is responsible for advancing the bitstream read cursor and is supposed to refuse the advance and leave the cursor untouched whenever it would move past the end of the input's heap-allocated byte buffer; instead it only logs a warning and still commits the out-of-range cursor position. Once that happens during an earlier field of the LWPOLYLINE entity, the peek helper reads from just past the true end of the buffer instead of stopping, producing the overflow.
